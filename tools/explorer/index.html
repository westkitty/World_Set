WORLD KIT · Brinefall Pump Hall — modular environment explorer

One concept, one construction language

Brine & Salt — a decommissioned sea-side desalination hall. Weathered cast concrete, oxide-red & teal enamel, brass fittings, standing brine under floor gratings and a sodium-lit beacon. Everything below is built only from the 40 catalogued kit pieces; the showcase is proof the modular system builds a finished location.

Visual invariants (every asset obeys)

  • 4.0 m bay grid; wall height 3.6 m; 0.95 m columns cap every grid junction.
  • Cast-concrete primary mass + enamel/brass/steel secondary; teal = treated brine, oxide = primer.
  • Every edge bevelled; no razor silhouettes; real-time poly budgets (foundation first).
  • Wear reads from the waterline up: salt streaks, tide rings, rust bleed under fittings.
  • Origins at design floor, footprint-centred; mechanical pivots on real hinges/axes.
  • Naming WK_[CAT]_[ASSET]_[VAR]_[NUM] — never Cube.004.

Pipeline (one command, fully regenerable)

  • tools/wk.py --stage textures — procedural PBR atlas, 14 families
  • --stage assets — build · audit · export 40 GLBs · manifest
  • --stage reuse — 3 layouts from the same pieces
  • --stage showcase — assemble hall · save master .blend
  • --stage stills | pano | walk — Cycles renders
  • --stage catalog — studio thumbs + contact sheets
